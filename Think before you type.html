What is Freedom of Expression?

Freedom of expression is the right of people to share their thoughts, opinions, and ideas. In the digital age, people can express themselves through social media, websites, messages, and other online platforms.

However, freedom of expression also comes with responsibility. We should express our opinions without using our freedom to hurt, threaten, or harass other people.

Key Issue: Online Harassment

What is Online Harassment?

Online harassment happens when someone repeatedly uses digital platforms to insult, threaten, embarrass, or harm another person. It can happen through social media, comments, messages, gaming platforms, and other online spaces.

Online harassment can make people afraid to express their opinions. It can also create an unsafe and negative online environment.

Impact on People and Society

  • People may become afraid to share their opinions.
  • Online communities can become toxic and unsafe.
  • Harassment can damage a person's confidence and well-being.
  • People may avoid participating in online discussions.
  • False or harmful posts can spread quickly online.

IT Connection

Information Technology plays an important role in freedom of expression because social media and digital platforms allow people to communicate quickly. IT professionals also help create systems for reporting harmful content, protecting users, and moderating online communities.

Technology can therefore be used not only to give people a voice but also to promote responsible and respectful communication.

Digital Responsibility: 3 Simple Tips

1. Think Before Posting

Ask yourself if your words could hurt or embarrass another person before you post or comment.

2. Report and Block

If you experience online harassment, use the platform's reporting and blocking tools and ask a trusted adult for help.

3. Respect Others

Share your opinions respectfully. Disagreeing with someone does not give us a reason to insult or harass them.
